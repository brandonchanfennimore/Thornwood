How to play Thornwood

Goal. Be the first to reach 30 renown. If nobody does by the end of round 20, the player with the most renown wins.

The forest. The map has 13 regions. Each is meadow, marsh or grove, and each has 1–2 building slots. Blue paths are rivers. The season moves on every round: meadow → marsh → grove.

Rule. Whoever has the most pieces (critters plus buildings) in a region rules it. A tie means nobody does.

Moving. Move any number of critters to an adjacent region. You must rule the region you leave or the one you enter.

Battle. The attacker rolls one d6 per critter (max 3) and the defender does the same. Each 4+ is a hit. Owls roll 2 dice each (max 4). A defender with a building there gets +1 die. A defender with no critters there gets no dice and takes +1 hit. Hits remove critters first, then buildings. Destroying a building earns 1 renown.

Cards. Every card belongs to a terrain or is a wild Moonlight card. Cards pay terrain costs and also have an effect when played: Rally, Forage, Hasten or Provision. Ambush cards trigger by themselves when you are attacked in a region of their terrain. At the end of your turn, draw 1 card, then discard down to 5.

Factions

Badger Clans: 3 actions. Each sett gives 1 stone at the start of your turn. Dig a sett in a region you rule for 1–3 stone, gaining renown. Once per turn, Muster adds 1 badger at each sett. At the end of your turn, score 1 per sett in the season's terrain. Badgers hit on 3+ when defending. You can discard a card to Quarry 1 stone.

Rabbit Warrens: 3 actions and lots of critters. At the start of your turn, Breed adds +1 rabbit in up to 4 regions you rule. Burst by paying a card matching a region where you have a rabbit to add 3 rabbits there. At the end of your turn, score 1 per region you rule in the season's terrain, +1 for every 3 regions you rule.

Owl Parliament: few but deadly. You get 2 actions, +1 per nest. Owls fly up to 2 regions and ignore rule. Once per turn, Hatch adds 1 owl at each nest. Build a nest with a matching card where you rule. Score 1 renown each time an attack removes enemy critters. Nests score 0/1/2/3 at the end of your turn.

Otter Company: actions cost coins. Moving costs 1 coin, attacking 2 and hiring 2 otters at a post 2. Moves along rivers ignore rule. You collect 1 coin plus 1 per post each turn, and a 1-coin toll whenever another faction moves into a region with your post. Open a trading post in a riverside region for 3 coins plus a matching card. At the end of your turn, each post scores 1, or 2 if another faction rules its region. You can sell cards for coins.
